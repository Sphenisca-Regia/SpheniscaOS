## Allowed without asking

- **Talking about SpheniscaOS**: articles, videos, reviews, talks, forum posts and comparisons may use the names to
  refer to the project.
- **Redistributing unmodified releases** under their name, for example packaging a tagged release for a distribution
  or mirroring it, as long as the content is the release as published.
- **"Runs SpheniscaOS"** or "works with SpheniscaOS" for a device, image or app that does.

## Not allowed

- **A modified distribution shipped as a product must use another name.** If you change SpheniscaOS or XX-WM and
  ship it to others (an image, a fork, a preinstalled phone), give it a name of your own that does not contain
  "Sphenisca" or "XX-WM". You may say truthfully that it is "based on SpheniscaOS".
- **No implied endorsement.** Do not use the names or the wordmark in a way that suggests Sphenisca Regia LLC
  sponsors, certifies or endorses you, your product or your company, unless it has agreed in writing.
- **No confusing names.** Do not use "Sphenisca" or a confusingly similar name for your own OS, app, company,
  domain or account.
- **Do not alter the wordmark** beyond scaling it.

## Questions

Ask the maintainers if you are unsure whether a use is allowed. This policy may be updated; the version in the
current release applies.
