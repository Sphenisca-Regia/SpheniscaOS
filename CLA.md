# SpheniscaOS contributor licence agreement

> **Draft.** Counsel reviews this text before the CLA bot is switched on. The licensor is Sphenisca Regia LLC.

This agreement has two forms. Sign the **individual** form if you contribute as yourself. If you contribute as part
of your job, or for any organization that may own what you write, that organization signs the **entity** form
(once), and you sign the individual form too.

Both forms follow the usual structure of contributor agreements used by large open-source projects: definitions, a
copyright licence, a patent licence, your promises, and what neither side owes the other. The wording is our own.

---

## Individual contributor licence agreement

Between you (the person named in the signature) and Sphenisca Regia LLC (the "Licensor"), which maintains
SpheniscaOS (the "Project").

### 1. Definitions

- **"Contribution"**: any work you submit to the Project (code, documentation, assets, tests, designs),
  intentionally, for inclusion in the Project. Something you mark "not a contribution" in writing is not one.
- **"Submit"**: send to the Project in any form, for example a pull request, a patch, an issue comment with code, or
  a message to the maintainers.

### 2. You keep your copyright

You keep the copyright in your Contributions. This agreement grants licences; it does not transfer ownership.

### 3. Copyright licence

You grant the Licensor, and anyone who receives software from the Licensor, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly
perform, sublicense and distribute your Contributions and derivative works of them.

**Relicensing.** The licence in this section includes the right of the Licensor to license your Contributions, as
part of the Project or on their own, under any terms, open source or not, now or later. The Licensor will also keep
making each Contribution available under the open-source licence of the part of the Project it was made to (for
first-party code, the Mozilla Public License 2.0) for as long as it distributes that part under an open-source licence.

### 4. Patent licence

You grant the Licensor, and anyone who receives software from the Licensor, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated here) patent licence to make, have made, use, sell, offer to
sell, import and otherwise transfer your Contributions, for the patent claims you can license that your Contribution
infringes on its own or combined with the Project as it was when you submitted it. If anyone sues claiming that the
Project or a Contribution infringes a patent, the patent licences that person received under this agreement end on
the date the suit is filed.

### 5. Your promises

You promise that:

1. you have the legal right to grant these licences;
2. each Contribution is your original work, or you say in the submission where it comes from and under which
   licence (for example "ported from project X, MIT licence"), and you do not knowingly submit anything taken from
   proprietary source code;
3. if your employer or another organization has rights in what you create, it has signed the entity form, or has
   allowed you in writing to contribute, or has waived its rights in your Contributions.

You will tell the Licensor if any of these stops being true.

### 6. What nobody owes

You are not expected to support your Contributions. Unless you agree otherwise in writing, you provide them "as
is", without warranties or conditions of any kind. The Licensor is not required to use any Contribution.

### 7. Signing

You sign by replying to the CLA bot on GitHub with the sentence it shows, from the account that submits your
Contributions. The bot records your GitHub user name, the date and the version of this text. One signature covers
all your Contributions to the Project.

## Entity contributor licence agreement

Between the organization named in the signature (the "Entity") and Sphenisca Regia LLC (the "Licensor"), which
maintains SpheniscaOS (the "Project").

### 1. Definitions

- **"Contribution"**: any work submitted to the Project by a Designated Contributor (below) that the Entity owns or
  has rights in, intentionally, for inclusion in the Project.
- **"Designated Contributor"**: a person the Entity names (by name and GitHub user name) as allowed to contribute on
  its behalf. The Entity keeps the list current by telling the Licensor of additions and removals.
- **"Entity"** includes organizations that control it, are controlled by it, or are under common control with it
  (control means owning more than half of the voting interest, or the power to direct its management).

### 2. The Entity keeps its copyright

The Entity keeps the copyright in its Contributions. This agreement grants licences; it does not transfer ownership.

### 3. Copyright licence

The Entity grants the Licensor, and anyone who receives software from the Licensor, a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly
display, publicly perform, sublicense and distribute its Contributions and derivative works of them.

**Relicensing.** The licence in this section includes the right of the Licensor to license the Contributions, as
part of the Project or on their own, under any terms, open source or not, now or later. The Licensor will also keep
making each Contribution available under the open-source licence of the part of the Project it was made to (for
first-party code, the Mozilla Public License 2.0) for as long as it distributes that part under an open-source licence.

### 4. Patent licence

The Entity grants the Licensor, and anyone who receives software from the Licensor, a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated here) patent licence to make, have made, use,
sell, offer to sell, import and otherwise transfer its Contributions, for the patent claims the Entity can license
that a Contribution infringes on its own or combined with the Project as it was when submitted. If anyone sues
claiming that the Project or a Contribution infringes a patent, the patent licences that person received under this
agreement end on the date the suit is filed.

### 5. The Entity's promises

The Entity promises that it has the legal right to grant these licences, that the person signing may bind it, and
that each Designated Contributor is allowed to submit Contributions on its behalf. It will tell the Licensor if any
of this stops being true.

### 6. What nobody owes

The Entity is not expected to support its Contributions. Unless it agrees otherwise in writing, it provides them "as
is", without warranties or conditions of any kind. The Licensor is not required to use any Contribution.

### 7. Signing

A person who may bind the Entity signs through the CLA bot on GitHub (or as the maintainers arrange) and gives the
Entity's legal name, address and the first list of Designated Contributors. Each Designated Contributor also signs
the individual form.
