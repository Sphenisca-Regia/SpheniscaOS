ringtone.ogg, unknown.ogg and notify.ogg are original sounds synthesized for
the XX apps from code: additive synthesis and exponential decays, no samples
and no recordings. ringtone.ogg and unknown.ogg are the xx-dialer ringtone
and unknown-caller tone; notify.ogg is the xx-calendar reminder chime.
